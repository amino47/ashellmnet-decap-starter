---
title: "Toki Pona is Cool, and so are Conlangs in general"
---
Recently, I've been trying to learn Toki Pona, a conlang that was designed with simplicity in mind. Depending who you talk to, it has between 120-180 words, though 120-137 is the most common range.
If you don't know what conlangs are, they are constructed languages (literally!). That means that they were designed by one or more people, and each word was specifically made, and unlike natural languages like English or Spanish, they weren't created by evolving over time as they were spoken, though some conlangs do evolve if they develop a community that speaks them. Esperanto is one of the more popular conlangs, and one that is more likely that you've heard of than Toki Pona.
